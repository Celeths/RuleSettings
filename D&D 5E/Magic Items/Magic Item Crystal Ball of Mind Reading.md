# Crystal Ball of Mind Reading

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)_

While touching this crystal orb, you can cast [[Spell Scrying]] (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you can cast [[Spell Detect Thoughts]] (save DC 17) targeting creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You don't need to concentrate on this [[Spell Detect Thoughts]] spell to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if the [[Spell Scrying]] spell ends.
