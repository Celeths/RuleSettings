# Eversmoking Bottle

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon_

As a [[Term Magic]] action, you can open or close this bottle.

Opening the bottle causes thick smoke to billow out, forming a cloud that fills a 60-foot [[Term Emanation]] originating from the bottle. The area within the smoke is [[Term Heavily Obscured]].

Each minute the bottle remains open, the size of the [[Term Emanation]] increases by 10 feet until it reaches its maximum size of 120 feet.

Closing the bottle causes the cloud to become fixed in place until it disperses after 10 minutes. A strong wind (such as that created by the [[Spell Gust of Wind]] spell) disperses the cloud after 1 minute.
