# Winged Boots

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

These boots have 4 charges and regain 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take a [[Term Magic]] action to expend 1 charge, gaining a [[Term Fly Speed]] of 30 feet for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.
