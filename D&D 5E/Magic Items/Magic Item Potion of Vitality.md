# Potion of Vitality

_Potion, Very Rare_

When you drink this potion, it removes any [[Term Exhaustion]] levels you have and ends the [[Term Poisoned]] condition on you. For the next 24 hours, you regain the maximum number of [[Term Hit Points]] for any Hit Point Die you spend.

This potion's crimson liquid regularly pulses with dull light, calling to mind a heartbeat.
