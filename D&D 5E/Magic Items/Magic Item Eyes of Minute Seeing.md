# Eyes of Minute Seeing

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon_

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, your vision improves significantly out to a range of 1 foot, granting you [[Term Darkvision]] within that range and [[Term Advantage]] on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to examine something within that range.
