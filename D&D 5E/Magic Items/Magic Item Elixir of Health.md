# Elixir of Health

_Potion, Rare_

When you drink this potion, you are cured of all magical contagions. In addition, the following conditions end on you: [[Term Blinded]], [[Term Deafened]], [[Term Paralyzed]], and [[Term Poisoned]].

The clear, red liquid has tiny bubbles of light in it.
