# Potion of Resistance

_Potion, Uncommon_

When you drink this potion, you have [[Term Resistance]] to one type of damage for 1 hour. The GM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the table.

|   1d10 | Damage Type   |
|--------|---------------|
|      1 | Acid          |
|      2 | Cold          |
|      3 | Fire          |
|      4 | Force         |
|      5 | Lightning     |
|      6 | Necrotic      |
|      7 | Poison        |
|      8 | Psychic       |
|      9 | Radiant       |
|     10 | Thunder       |
