# Boots of Speed

_Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)_

While you wear these boots, you can take a [[Term Bonus Action]] to click the boots' heels together. If you do, the boots double your [[Term Speed]], and any creature that makes an Opportunity [[Term Attack]] against you has [[Term Disadvantage]] on the attack roll. If you click your heels together again, you end the effect.

When you've used the boots' property for a total of 10 minutes, the magic ceases to function for you until you finish a [[Term Long Rest]].
