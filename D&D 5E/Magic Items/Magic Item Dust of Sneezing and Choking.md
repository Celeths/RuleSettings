# Dust of Sneezing and Choking

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon_

Found in a small container, this powder resembles [[Magic Item Dust of Disappearance]], and [[Spell Identify]] reveals it to be such. There is enough of it for one use.

As a [[Term Utilize]] action, you can throw the dust into the air, forcing yourself and every creature in a 30-foot [[Term Emanation]] originating from you to make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. Constructs, Elementals, Oozes, Plants, and Undead succeed on the save automatically.

On a failed save, a creature begins sneezing uncontrollably; it has the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition and is suffocating. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. The effect also ends on any creature targeted by a [[Spell Lesser Restoration]] spell.
