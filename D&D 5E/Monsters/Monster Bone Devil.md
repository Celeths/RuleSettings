# Bone Devil

_Large Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil_

- **AC** 16
- **Initiative** +7 (17)
- **HP** 161 (17d10 + 68)
- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     18 |     16 |     18 |     13 |     14 |     16 |
| **Modifier**     |     +4 |     +3 |     +4 |     +1 |     +2 |     +3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +8 |     +3 |     +4 |     +5 |     +6 |     +7 |

- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +6
- **Resistances** Cold
- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Darkvision]] 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical [[Term Darkness]]); [[Term Passive Perception]] 12
- **Languages** Infernal; telepathy 120 ft.
- **CR** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)

## Traits

_**Diabolical Restoration.**_ If the devil dies outside the Nine Hells, its body disappears in sulfurous smoke, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its [[Term Hit Points]] somewhere in the Nine Hells.

_**Magic Resistance.**_ The devil has [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The devil makes two Claw attacks and one Infernal Sting attack.

_**Claw.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.

_**Infernal Sting.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_  15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) Poison damage, and the target has the [[Term Poisoned]] condition until the start of the devil's next turn. While Poisoned, the target can't regain [[Term Hit Points]].
