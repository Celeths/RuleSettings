# Swarm of Piranhas

_Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned_

- **AC** 13
- **Initiative** +3 (13)
- **HP** 28 (8d8 - 8)
- **Speed** 5 ft., Swim 40 ft.

- **Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing

- **Languages** None
- **CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny piranha. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

_**Water Breathing.**_ The swarm can breathe only underwater.

## Actions

_**Bites.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5 (with [[Term Advantage]] if the target doesn't have all its [[Term Hit Points]]), reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage, or 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]].
