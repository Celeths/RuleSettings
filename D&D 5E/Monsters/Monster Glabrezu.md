# Glabrezu

_Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil_

- **AC** 17
- **Initiative** +6 (16)
- **HP** 189 (18d10 + 90)
- **Speed** 40 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     20 |     15 |     21 |     19 |     17 |     16 |
| **Modifier**     |     +5 |     +2 |     +5 |     +4 |     +3 |     +3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +9 |     +2 |     +9 |     +4 |     +7 |     +7 |

- **Skills** Deception +7, Perception +7
- **Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
- **Immunities** Poison; [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Truesight]] 120 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 17
- **Languages** Abyssal; telepathy 120 ft.
- **CR** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)

## Traits

_**Demonic Restoration.**_ If the glabrezu dies outside the Abyss, its body dissolves into ichor, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its [[Term Hit Points]] somewhere in the Abyss.

_**Magic Resistance.**_ The glabrezu has [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The glabrezu makes two Pincer attacks and uses Pummel or Spellcasting.

_**Pincer.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +9, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 15) from one of two pincers.

_**Pummel.**_ _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 17, one creature [[Term Grappled]] by the glabrezu. _Failure:_ 15 (3d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. _Success:_ Half damage.

_**Spellcasting.**_ The glabrezu casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):

- **At Will:** [[Term Darkness]], [[Spell Detect Magic]], [[Spell Dispel Magic]]
- **1/Day Each:** [[Spell Confusion]], [[Spell Fly]], [[Spell Power Word Stun]]
