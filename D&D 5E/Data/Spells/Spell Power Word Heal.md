# Power Word Heal

_Level 9 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its [[Term Hit Points]]. If the creature has the [[Term Charmed]], [[Term Frightened]], [[Term Paralyzed]], [[Term Poisoned]], or [[Term Stunned]] condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [[Term Prone]] condition, it can use its [[Term Reaction]] to stand up.
