# Fog Cloud

_Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] of fog centered on a point within range. The Sphere is [[Term Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by [[Spell Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The fog's radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.
