# Power Word Kill

_Level 9 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You compel one creature you can see within range to die. If the target has 100 [[Term Hit Points]] or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes 12d12 Psychic damage.
