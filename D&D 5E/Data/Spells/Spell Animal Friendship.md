# Animal Friendship

_Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

[[Term Target]] a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Term Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
