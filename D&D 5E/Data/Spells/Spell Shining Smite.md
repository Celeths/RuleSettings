# Shining Smite

_Level 2 Transmutation (Paladin)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]], which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Term Unarmed Strike]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds [[Term Bright Light]] in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have [[Term Advantage]], and it can't benefit from the [[Term Invisible]] condition.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
