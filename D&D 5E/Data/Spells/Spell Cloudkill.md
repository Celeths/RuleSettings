# Cloudkill

_Level 5 Conjuration (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] of yellow-green fog centered on a point within range. The fog lasts for the duration or until strong wind (such as the one created by [[Spell Gust of Wind]]) disperses it, ending the spell. Its area is [[Term Heavily Obscured]].

Each creature in the [[Term Sphere]] makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 5d8 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the Sphere moves into its space and when it enters the Sphere or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The [[Term Sphere]] moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.
