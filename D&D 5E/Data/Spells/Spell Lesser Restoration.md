# Lesser Restoration

_Level 2 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: [[Term Blinded]], [[Term Deafened]], [[Term Paralyzed]], or [[Term Poisoned]].
