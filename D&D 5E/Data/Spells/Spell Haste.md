# Haste

_Level 3 Transmutation (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a shaving of licorice root)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose a willing creature that you can see within range. Until the spell ends, the target's [[Term Speed]] is doubled, it gains a +2 bonus to [[Term Armor Class]], it has [[Term Advantage]] on Dexterity saving throws, and it gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used to take only the [[Term Attack]] (one attack only), [[Term Dash]], [[Term Disengage]], [[Term Hide]], or [[Term Utilize]] action.

When the spell ends, the target is [[Term Incapacitated]] and has a [[Term Speed]] of 0 until the end of its next turn, as a wave of lethargy washes over it.
