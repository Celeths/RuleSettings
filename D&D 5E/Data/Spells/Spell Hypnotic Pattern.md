# Hypnotic Pattern

_Level 3 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a pinch of confetti)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 30-foot [[Term Cube]] within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Term Charmed]] condition for the duration. While Charmed, the creature has the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition and a [[Term Speed]] of 0.

The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses an action to shake the creature out of its stupor.
