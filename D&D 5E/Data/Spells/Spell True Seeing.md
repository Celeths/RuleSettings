# True Seeing

_Level 6 Divination (Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (mushroom powder worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [[Term Truesight]] with a range of 120 feet.
