# Irresistible Dance

_Level 6 Enchantment (Bard, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the target dances comically until the end of its next turn, during which it must spend all its movement to dance in place.

On a failed save, the target has the [[Term Charmed]] condition for the duration. While Charmed, the target dances comically, must use all its movement to dance in place, and has [[Term Disadvantage]] on Dexterity saving throws and attack rolls, and other creatures have [[Term Advantage]] on attack rolls against it. On each of its turns, the target can take an action to collect itself and repeat the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
