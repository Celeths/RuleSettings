# Daylight

_Level 3 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, sunlight spreads from a point within range and fills a 60-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]]. The sunlight's area is [[Term Bright Light]] and sheds [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet.

Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the sunlight to fill a 60-foot [[Term Emanation]] originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the sunlight.

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[Term Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.
