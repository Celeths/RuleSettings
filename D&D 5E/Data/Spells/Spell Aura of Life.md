# Aura of Life

_Level 4 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Term Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Term Resistance]] to Necrotic damage, and your Hit Point maximums can't be reduced. If an ally with 0 [[Term Hit Points]] starts its turn in the aura, that ally regains 1 Hit Point.
