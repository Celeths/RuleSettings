# Purify Food and Drink

_Level 1 Transmutation (Cleric, Druid, Paladin)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]] or [[Term Ritual]]
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical food and drink in a 5-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] centered on a point within range.
