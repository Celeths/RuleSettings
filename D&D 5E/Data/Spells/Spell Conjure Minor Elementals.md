# Conjure Minor Elementals

_Level 4 Conjuration (Druid, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You conjure spirits from the Elemental Planes that flit around you in a 15-foot [[Term Emanation]] for the duration. Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals an extra 2d8 damage when you hit a creature in the Emanation. This damage is Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning (your choice when you make the attack).

In addition, the ground in the [[Term Emanation]] is [[Term Difficult Terrain]] for your enemies.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.
