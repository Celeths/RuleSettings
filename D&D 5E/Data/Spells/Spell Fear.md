# Fear

_Level 3 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a white feather)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Each creature in a 30-foot [[Term Cone]] must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or drop whatever it is holding and have the [[Term Frightened]] condition for the duration.

A [[Term Frightened]] creature takes the [[Term Dash]] action and moves away from you by the safest route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move. If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn't have line of sight to you, the creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.
