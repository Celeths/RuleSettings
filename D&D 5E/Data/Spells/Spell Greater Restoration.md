# Greater Restoration

_Level 5 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and magically remove one of the following effects from it:

- 1 [[Term Exhaustion]] level
- The [[Term Charmed]] or [[Term Petrified]] condition
- A curse, including the target's [[Term Attunement]] to a cursed magic item
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores
- Any reduction to the target's Hit Point maximum
