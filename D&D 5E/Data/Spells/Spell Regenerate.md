# Regenerate

_Level 7 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Druid)_

- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a prayer wheel)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

A creature you touch regains 4d8 + 15 [[Term Hit Points]]. For the duration, the target regains 1 Hit Point at the start of each of its turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.
