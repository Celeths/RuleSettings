# Ray of Sickness

_Level 1 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You shoot a greenish ray at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Poison damage and has the [[Term Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
