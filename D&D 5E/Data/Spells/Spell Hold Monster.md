# Hold Monster

_Level 5 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Term Paralyzed]] condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 5.
