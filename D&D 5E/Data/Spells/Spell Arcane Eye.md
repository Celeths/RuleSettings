# Arcane Eye

_Level 4 Divination (Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of bat fur)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You create an [[Term Invisible]], invulnerable eye within range that hovers for the duration. You mentally receive visual information from the eye, which can see in every direction. It also has [[Term Darkvision]] with a range of 30 feet.

As a [[Term Bonus Action]], you can move the eye up to 30 feet in any direction. A solid barrier blocks the eye's movement, but the eye can pass through an opening as small as 1 inch in diameter.
