# Protection from Evil and Good

_Level 1 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a flask of Holy Water worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]] up to 10 minutes

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is protected against creatures that are Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, or Undead. The protection grants several benefits. Creatures of those types have [[Term Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against the target. The target also can't be possessed by or gain the [[Term Charmed]] or [[Term Frightened]] conditions from them. If the target is already possessed, Charmed, or Frightened by such a creature, the target has [[Term Advantage]] on any new saving throw against the relevant effect.
