# Color Spray

_Level 1 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colorful sand)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Term Cone]] originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[Term Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.
