# Invisibility

_Level 2 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (an eyelash in gum arabic)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

A creature you touch has the [[Term Invisible]] condition until the spell ends. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
