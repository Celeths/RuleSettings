# See Invisibility

_Level 2 Divination (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the [[Term Invisible]] condition as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Creatures and objects there appear ghostly.
