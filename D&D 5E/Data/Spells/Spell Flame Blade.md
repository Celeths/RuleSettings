# Flame Blade

_Level 2 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a sumac leaf)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You evoke a fiery blade in your free hand. The blade is similar in size and shape to a scimitar, and it lasts for the duration. If you let go of the blade, it disappears, but you can evoke it again as a [[Term Bonus Action]].

As a [[Term Magic]] action, you can make a melee spell attack with the fiery blade. On a hit, the target takes Fire damage equal to 3d6 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

The flaming blade sheds [[Term Bright Light]] in a 10-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
