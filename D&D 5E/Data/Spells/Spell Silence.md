# Silence

_Level 2 Illusion (Bard, Cleric, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]] or [[Term Ritual]]
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the Sphere has [[Term Immunity]] to Thunder damage, and creatures have the [[Term Deafened]] condition while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a Verbal component is impossible there.
