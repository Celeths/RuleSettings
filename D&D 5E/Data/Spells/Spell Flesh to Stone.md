# Flesh to Stone

_Level 6 Transmutation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a cockatrice feather)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You attempt to turn one creature that you can see within range into stone. The target makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it has the [[Term Restrained]] condition for the duration. On a successful save, its [[Term Speed]] is 0 until the start of your next turn. Constructs automatically succeed on the save.

A [[Term Restrained]] target makes another Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If it successfully saves against this spell three times, the spell ends. If it fails its saves three times, it is turned to stone and has the [[Term Petrified]] condition for the duration. The successes and failures needn't be consecutive; keep track of both until the target collects three of a kind.

If you maintain your [[Term Concentration]] on this spell for the entire possible duration, the target is [[Term Petrified]] until the condition is ended by [[Spell Greater Restoration]] or similar magic.
