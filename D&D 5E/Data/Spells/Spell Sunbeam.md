# Sunbeam

_Level 6 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a magnifying glass)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You launch a sunbeam in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long [[Term Line]]. Each creature in the Line makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Radiant damage and has the [[Term Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Until the spell ends, you can take a [[Term Magic]] action to create a new [[Term Line]] of radiance.

For the duration, a mote of brilliant radiance shines above you. It sheds [[Term Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight.
