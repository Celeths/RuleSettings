# Spike Growth

_Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (seven thorns)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

The ground in a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] centered on a point within range sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes [[Term Difficult Terrain]] for the duration. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels.

The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area when the spell is cast must take a [[Term Search]] action and succeed on a Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check against your spell save DC to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.
