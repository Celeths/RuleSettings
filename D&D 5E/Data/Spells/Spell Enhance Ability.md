# Enhance Ability

_Level 2 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (fur or a feather)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You touch a creature and choose Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. For the duration, the target has [[Term Advantage]] on ability checks using the chosen ability.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2. You can choose a different ability for each target.
