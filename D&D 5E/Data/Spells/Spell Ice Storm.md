# Ice Storm

_Level 4 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 300 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a mitten)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Hail falls in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high [[Term Cylinder]] centered on a point within range. Each creature in the Cylinder makes a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage and 4d6 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Hailstones turn ground in the [[Term Cylinder]] into [[Term Difficult Terrain]] until the end of your next turn.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The Bludgeoning damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 4.
