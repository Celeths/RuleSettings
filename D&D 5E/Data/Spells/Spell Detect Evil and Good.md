# Detect Evil and Good

_Level 1 Divination (Cleric, Paladin)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

For the duration, you sense the location of any Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead within 30 feet of yourself. You also sense whether the [[Spell Hallow]] spell is active there and, if so, where.

The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
