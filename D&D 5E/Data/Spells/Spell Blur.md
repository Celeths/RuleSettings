# Blur

_Level 2 Illusion (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Your body becomes blurred. For the duration, any creature has [[Term Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you. An attacker is immune to this effect if it perceives you with [[Term Blindsight]] or [[Term Truesight]].
