# Misty Step

_Level 2 Conjuration (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Briefly surrounded by silvery mist, you teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
