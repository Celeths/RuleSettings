# Ability Score and Modifier

A creature has six ability scores — Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma — each of which has a corresponding modifier. Add the modifier when you make a [[Term D20 Test]] with the corresponding ability or when a rule asks you to do so. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("The Six Abilities").
