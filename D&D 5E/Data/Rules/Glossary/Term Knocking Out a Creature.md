# Knocking Out a Creature

When you would reduce a creature to 0 [[Term Hit Points]] with a melee attack, you can instead reduce the creature to 1 Hit Point. The creature then has the [[Term Unconscious]] condition and starts a [[Term Short Rest]].

The creature remains [[Term Unconscious]] until it regains any [[Term Hit Points]] or until someone uses an action to administer first aid to it, which requires a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check.
