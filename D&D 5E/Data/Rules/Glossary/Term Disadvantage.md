# Disadvantage

If you have Disadvantage on a D20 Test, roll two d20s and use the lower roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one Disadvantage, and [[Term Advantage]] and Disadvantage on the same roll cancel each other. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("[[Term D20 Test]]s").
