# Incapacitated \[Condition\]

While you have the Incapacitated condition, you experience the following effects.

_**Inactive.**_ You can't take any action, [[Term Bonus Action]], or [[Term Reaction]].

_**No Concentration.**_ Your [[Term Concentration]] is broken.

_**Speechless.**_ You can't speak.

_**Surprised.**_ If you're Incapacitated when you roll [[Term Initiative]], you have [[Term Disadvantage]] on the roll.
