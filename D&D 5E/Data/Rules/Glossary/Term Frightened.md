# Frightened \[Condition\]

While you have the Frightened condition, you experience the following effects.

_**Ability Checks and Attacks Affected.**_ You have [[Term Disadvantage]] on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is within line of sight.

_**Can't Approach.**_ You can't willingly move closer to the source of fear.
