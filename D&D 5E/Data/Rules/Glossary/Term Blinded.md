# Blinded \[Condition\]

While you have the Blinded condition, you experience the following effects.

_**Can't See.**_ You can't see and automatically fail any ability check that requires sight.

_**Attacks Affected.**_ [[Term Attack]] rolls against you have [[Term Advantage]], and your attack rolls have [[Term Disadvantage]].
