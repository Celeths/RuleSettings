# Unconscious \[Condition\]

While you have the Unconscious condition, you experience the following effects.

_**Inert.**_ You have the [[Term Incapacitated]] and [[Term Prone]] conditions, and you drop whatever you're holding. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.

_**Speed 0.**_ Your [[Term Speed]] is 0 and can't increase.

_**Attacks Affected.**_ [[Term Attack]] rolls against you have [[Term Advantage]].

_**Saving Throws Affected.**_ You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

_**Automatic Critical Hits.**_ Any attack roll that hits you is a [[Term Critical Hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.

_**Unaware.**_ You're unaware of your surroundings.
