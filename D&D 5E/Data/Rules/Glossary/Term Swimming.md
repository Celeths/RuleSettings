# Swimming

While you're swimming, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in [[Term Difficult Terrain]]). You ignore this extra cost if you have a [[Term Swim Speed]] and use it to swim. At the GM's option, moving any distance in rough water might require a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check.
