# Proficiency

If you have proficiency with something, you can add your Proficiency Bonus to any [[Term D20 Test]] you make using that thing. A creature might have proficiency in a skill or saving throw or with a weapon or tool. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("Proficiency").
