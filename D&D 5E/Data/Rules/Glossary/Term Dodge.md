# Dodge \[Action\]

If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has [[Term Disadvantage]] if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with [[Term Advantage]].

You lose these benefits if you have the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition or if your [[Term Speed]] is 0.
