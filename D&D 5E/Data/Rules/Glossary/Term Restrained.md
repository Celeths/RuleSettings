# Restrained \[Condition\]

While you have the Restrained condition, you experience the following effects.

_**Speed 0.**_ Your [[Term Speed]] is 0 and can't increase.

_**Attacks Affected.**_ [[Term Attack]] rolls against you have [[Term Advantage]], and your attack rolls have [[Term Disadvantage]].

_**Saving Throws Affected.**_ You have [[Term Disadvantage]] on Dexterity saving throws.
