# Initiative

Initiative determines the order of turns during combat. The combat rules in "Playing the Game" explain how to roll Initiative.

Sometimes a GM might have combatants use their Initiative scores instead of rolling Initiative. Your Initiative score equals 10 plus your Dexterity modifier. If you have [[Term Advantage]] on Initiative rolls, increase your Initiative score by 5. If you have [[Term Disadvantage]] on those rolls, decrease that score by 5. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
