# Cursed Items

A magic item's description specifies whether it bears a curse. Most methods of identifying items, including the [[Spell Identify]] spell, fail to reveal such a curse.

[[Term Attunement]] to a cursed item can't be ended voluntarily unless the curse is broken first, such as with a [[Spell Remove Curse]] spell.
