## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny snake. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Bites.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage — or 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]] — plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.
