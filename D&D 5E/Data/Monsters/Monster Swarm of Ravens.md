# Swarm of Ravens

_Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned_

- **AC** 12
- **Initiative** +2 (12)
- **HP** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 50 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |      6 |     14 |     12 |      5 |     12 |      6 |
| **Modifier**     |     -2 |     +2 |     +1 |     -3 |     +1 |     -2 |
| **Saving Throw** |     -2 |     +2 |     +1 |     -3 |     +1 |     -2 |

- **Skills** Perception +5
- **Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** [[Term Charmed]], [[Term Frightened]], [[Term Grappled]], [[Term Paralyzed]], [[Term Petrified]], [[Term Prone]], [[Term Restrained]], [[Term Stunned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Passive Perception]] 15
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Beaks.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]].

_**Cacophony (Recharge 6).**_ _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. _Failure:_ The target has the [[Term Deafened]] condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Deafened, the target also has [[Term Disadvantage]] on ability checks and attack rolls.
