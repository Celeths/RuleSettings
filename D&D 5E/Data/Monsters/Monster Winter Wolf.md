## Traits

_**Pack Tactics.**_ The wolf has [[Term Advantage]] on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition.

## Actions

_**Bite.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Prone]] condition.

_**Cold Breath (Recharge 5-6).**_ _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot [[Term Cone]]. _Failure:_ 18 (4d8) Cold damage. _Success:_ Half damage.
