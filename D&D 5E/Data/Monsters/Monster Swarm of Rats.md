## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny rat. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Bites.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +2, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (2d4) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]].
