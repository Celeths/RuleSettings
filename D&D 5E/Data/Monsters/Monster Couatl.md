# Couatl

_Medium Celestial, Lawful Good_

- **AC** 19
- **Initiative** +5 (15)
- **HP** 60 (8d8 + 24)
- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 90 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     16 |     20 |     17 |     18 |     20 |     18 |
| **Modifier**     |     +3 |     +5 |     +3 |     +4 |     +5 |     +4 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +3 |     +5 |     +5 |     +4 |     +7 |     +4 |

- **Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** Psychic, Radiant
- **Senses** [[Term Truesight]] 120 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 15
- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
- **CR** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Shielded Mind.**_ The couatl's thoughts can't be read by any means, and other creatures can communicate with it telepathically only if it allows them.

## Actions

_**Bite.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (1d12 + 5) Piercing damage, and the target has the [[Term Poisoned]] condition until the end of the couatl's next turn.

_**Constrict.**_ _Strength Saving Throw:_ DC 15, one Medium or smaller creature the couatl can see within 5 feet. _Failure:_ 8 (1d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. The target has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13), and it has the [[Term Restrained]] condition until the grapple ends.

_**Spellcasting.**_ The couatl casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):

- **At Will:** [[Spell Detect Evil and Good]], [[Spell Detect Magic]], [[Spell Detect Thoughts]], [[Spell Shapechange]] (Beast or Humanoid form only, no [[Term Temporary Hit Points]] gained from the spell, and no [[Term Concentration]] or Temporary [[Term Hit Points]] required to maintain the spell)
- **1/Day Each:** [[Spell Create Food and Water]], [[Spell Dream]], [[Spell Greater Restoration]], [[Spell Scrying]], [[Spell Sleep]]

## Bonus Actions

_**Divine Aid (2/Day).**_ The couatl casts [[Spell Bless]], [[Spell Lesser Restoration]], or [[Spell Sanctuary]], requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
