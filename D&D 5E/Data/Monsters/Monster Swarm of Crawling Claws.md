## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Swarm of Grasping Hands.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 20 (4d8 + 2) Necrotic damage, or 11 (2d8 + 2) Necrotic damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]]. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Prone]] condition.
