# Bugbear Stalker

_Medium Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil_

- **AC** 15
- **Initiative** +2 (12)
- **HP** 65 (10d8 + 20)
- **Speed** 30 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     17 |     14 |     14 |     11 |     12 |     11 |
| **Modifier**     |     +3 |     +2 |     +2 |     +0 |     +1 |     +0 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +3 |     +2 |     +4 |     +0 |     +3 |     +0 |

- **Skills** Stealth +6, Survival +3
- **Gear** Chain Shirt, Javelins (6), Morningstar
- **Senses** [[Term Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 11
- **Languages** Common, Goblin
- **CR** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Abduct.**_ The bugbear needn't spend extra movement to move a creature it is grappling.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The bugbear makes two Javelin or Morningstar attacks.

_**Javelin.**_ _Melee or Ranged [[Term Attack Roll]]:_ +5, reach 10 ft. or range 30/120 ft. _Hit:_ 13 (3d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

_**Morningstar.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5 (with [[Term Advantage]] if the target is [[Term Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.

## Bonus Actions

_**Quick Grapple.**_ _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one Medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. _Failure:_ The target has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13).
