# Swarm of Insects

_Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned_

- **AC** 11
- **Initiative** +1 (11)
- **HP** 19 (3d8 + 6)
- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb or Fly 20 ft. (GM's choice)

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |      3 |     13 |     14 |      1 |      7 |      1 |
| **Modifier**     |     -4 |     +1 |     +2 |     -5 |     -2 |     -5 |
| **Saving Throw** |     -4 |     +1 |     +2 |     -5 |     -2 |     -5 |

- **Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** [[Term Charmed]], [[Term Frightened]], [[Term Grappled]], [[Term Paralyzed]], [[Term Petrified]], [[Term Prone]], [[Term Restrained]], [[Term Stunned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Blindsight]] 30 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 8
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Spider Climb.**_ If the swarm has a [[Term Climb Speed]], the swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Bites.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +3, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 6 (2d4 + 1) Poison damage, or 3 (1d4 + 1) Poison damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]].
