_Large Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned_

- **AC** 12
- **Initiative** +2 (12)
- **HP** 11 (2d10)
- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft.

- **Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** [[Term Charmed]], [[Term Frightened]], [[Term Grappled]], [[Term Paralyzed]], [[Term Petrified]], [[Term Prone]], [[Term Restrained]], [[Term Stunned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Blindsight]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 11
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Swarm.**_ The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny bat. The swarm can't regain [[Term Hit Points]] or gain [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

_**Bites.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (2d4) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[Term Bloodied]].
