# Ice Mephit

_Small Elemental, Neutral Evil_

- **AC** 11
- **Initiative** +1 (11)
- **HP** 21 (6d6)
- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |      7 |     13 |     10 |      9 |     11 |     12 |
| **Modifier**     |     -2 |     +1 |     +0 |     -1 |     +0 |     +1 |
| **Saving Throw** |     -2 |     +1 |     +0 |     -1 |     +0 |     +1 |

- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +3
- **Vulnerabilities** Fire
- **Immunities** Cold, Poison; [[Term Exhaustion]], [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 12
- **Languages** Primordial (Aquan, Auran)
- **CR** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Death Burst.**_ The mephit explodes when it dies. _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 10, each creature in a 5-foot [[Term Emanation]] originating from the mephit. _Failure:_ 5 (2d4) Cold damage. _Success:_ Half damage.

## Actions

_**Claw.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +3, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 3 (1d4 + 1) Slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) Cold damage.

_**Fog Cloud (1/Day).**_ The mephit casts [[Spell Fog Cloud]], requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.

_**Frost Breath (Recharge 6).**_ _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 10, each creature in a 15-foot [[Term Cone]]. _Failure:_ 7 (3d4) Cold damage. _Success:_ Half damage.
