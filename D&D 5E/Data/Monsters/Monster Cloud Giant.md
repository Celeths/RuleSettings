# Cloud Giant

_Huge Giant, Neutral_

- **AC** 14
- **Initiative** +4 (14)
- **HP** 200 (16d12 + 96)
- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 20 ft. (hover)

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     27 |     10 |     22 |     12 |     16 |     16 |
| **Modifier**     |     +8 |     +0 |     +6 |     +1 |     +3 |     +3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +8 |     +0 |    +10 |     +1 |     +7 |     +3 |

- **Skills** Insight +7, Perception +11
- **Senses** [[Term Passive Perception]] 21
- **Languages** Common, Giant
- **CR** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The giant makes two attacks, using Thunderous Mace or Thundercloud in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast [[Spell Fog Cloud]].

_**Thunderous Mace.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +12, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 21 (3d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Thunder damage.

_**Thundercloud.**_ _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +12, range 240 ft. _Hit:_ 18 (3d6 + 8) Thunder damage, and the target has the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn.

_**Spellcasting.**_ The giant casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):

- **At Will:** [[Spell Detect Magic]], [[Spell Fog Cloud]], [[Spell Light]]
- **1/Day Each:** [[Spell Control Weather]], [[Spell Gaseous Form]], [[Spell Telekinesis]]

## Bonus Actions

_**Misty Step.**_ The giant casts the [[Spell Misty Step]] spell, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
