# Roc

_Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned_

- **AC** 15
- **Initiative** +8 (18)
- **HP** 248 (16d20 + 80)
- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 120 ft.

|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     28 |     10 |     20 |      3 |     10 |      9 |

| **Saving Throw** |     +9 |     +4 |     +5 |     -4 |     +4 |     -1 |

- **Skills** Perception +8
- **Senses** [[Term Passive Perception]] 18
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The roc makes two Beak attacks. It can replace one attack with a Talons attack.

_**Beak.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +13, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 28 (3d12 + 9) Piercing damage.

_**Talons.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +13, reach 5 ft. Hit: 23 (4d6 + 9) Slashing damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 19) from both talons, and it has the [[Term Restrained]] condition until the grapple ends.

## Bonus Actions

_**Swoop (Recharge 5-6).**_ If the roc has a creature [[Term Grappled]], the roc flies up to half its [[Term Fly Speed]] without provoking [[Term Opportunity Attacks]] and drops that creature.
