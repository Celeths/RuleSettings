# Giant Octopus

_Large Beast, Unaligned_

- **AC** 11
- **Initiative** +1 (11)
- **HP** 45 (7d10 + 7)
- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 60 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     17 |     13 |     13 |      5 |     10 |      4 |
| **Modifier**     |     +3 |     +1 |     +1 |     -3 |     +0 |     -3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +3 |     +1 |     +1 |     -3 |     +0 |     -3 |

- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
- **Senses** [[Term Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 14
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Water Breathing.**_ The octopus can breathe only underwater. It can hold its breath for 1 hour outside water.

## Actions

_**Tentacles.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) from all eight tentacles. While Grappled, the target has the [[Term Restrained]] condition.

## Reactions

_**Ink Cloud (1/Day).**_ _Trigger:_ The octopus takes damage while underwater. _Response:_ The octopus releases ink that fills a 10-foot [[Term Cube]] centered on itself, and the octopus moves up to its [[Term Swim Speed]]. The Cube is [[Term Heavily Obscured]] for 1 minute or until a strong current or similar effect disperses the ink.
