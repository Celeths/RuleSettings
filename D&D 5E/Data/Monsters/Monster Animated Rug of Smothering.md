# Animated Rug of Smothering

_Large Construct, Unaligned_

- **AC** 12
- **Initiative** +4 (14)
- **HP** 27 (5d10)
- **Speed** 10 ft.

| **Saving Throw** |     +3 |     +2 |     +0 |     -5 |     -4 |     -5 |

- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; [[Term Charmed]], [[Term Deafened]], [[Term Exhaustion]], [[Term Frightened]], [[Term Paralyzed]], [[Term Petrified]], [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Blindsight]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 6
- **Languages** None
- **CR** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)

## Actions

_**Smother.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the rug can give it the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) instead of dealing damage. Until the grapple ends, the target has the [[Term Blinded]] and [[Term Restrained]] conditions, is suffocating, and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The rug can smother only one creature at a time.

While grappling the target, the rug can't take this action, the rug halves the damage it takes (round down), and the target takes the same amount of damage.
