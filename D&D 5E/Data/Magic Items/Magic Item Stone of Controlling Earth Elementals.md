# Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

_Wondrous Item, Rare_

While touching this 5-pound stone to the ground, you can take a [[Term Magic]] action to summon an **[[Monster Earth Elemental]]**. The elemental appears in an unoccupied space you choose within 30 feet of yourself, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[Term Initiative]] count. The elemental disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a [[Term Bonus Action]]. The stone can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
