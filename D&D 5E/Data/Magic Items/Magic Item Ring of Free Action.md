# Ring of Free Action

_Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)_

While you wear this ring, [[Term Difficult Terrain]] doesn't cost you extra movement. In addition, magic can neither reduce any of your Speeds nor cause you to have the [[Term Paralyzed]] or [[Term Restrained]] condition.
