# Potion of Clairvoyance

_Potion, Rare_

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Spell Clairvoyance]] spell (no [[Term Concentration]] required).

An eyeball bobs in this potion's yellowish liquid but vanishes when the potion is opened.
