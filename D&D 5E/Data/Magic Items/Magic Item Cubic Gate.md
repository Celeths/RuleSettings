# Cubic Gate

_Wondrous Item, Legendary_

This cube is 3 inches across and radiates palpable magical energy. The six sides of the cube are each keyed to a different plane of existence, one of which is the Material Plane. The other sides are linked to planes determined by the GM.

The cube has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a [[Term Magic]] action, you can expend 1 of the cube's charges to cast one of the following spells using the cube.

_**Gate.**_ Pressing one side of the cube, you cast [[Spell Gate]], opening a portal to the plane of existence keyed to that side.

_**Plane Shift.**_ Pressing one side of the cube twice, you cast [[Spell Plane Shift]], transporting the targets to the plane of existence keyed to that side.
