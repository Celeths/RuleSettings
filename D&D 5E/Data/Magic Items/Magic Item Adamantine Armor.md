# Adamantine Armor

_Armor (Any Medium or Heavy, Except Hide Armor), Uncommon_

This suit of armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any [[Term Critical Hit]] against you becomes a normal hit.
