# Crystal Ball of True Seeing

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)_

While touching this crystal orb, you can cast [[Spell Scrying]] (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you have [[Term Truesight]] with a range of 120 feet centered on the spell's sensor.
