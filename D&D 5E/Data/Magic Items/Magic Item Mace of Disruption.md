# Mace of Disruption

_Weapon (Mace), Rare (Requires Attunement)_

When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage. If the target has 25 [[Term Hit Points]] or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the [[Term Frightened]] condition until the end of your next turn.

_**Light.**_ While you hold this weapon, it sheds [[Term Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet.
