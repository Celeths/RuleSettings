# Ring of Evasion

_Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)_

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you fail a Dexterity saving throw while wearing the ring, you can take a [[Term Reaction]] to expend 1 charge to succeed on that save instead.
