# Cloak of Elvenkind

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

While you wear this cloak, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive you have [[Term Disadvantage]], and you have [[Term Advantage]] on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
