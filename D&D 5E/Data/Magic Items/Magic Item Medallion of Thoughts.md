# Medallion of Thoughts

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

The medallion has 5 charges. While wearing it, you can expend 1 charge to cast [[Spell Detect Thoughts]] (save DC 13) from it. The medallion regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.
