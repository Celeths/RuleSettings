# Potion of Climbing

_Potion, Common_

When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Term Climb Speed]] equal to your [[Term Speed]] for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[Term Advantage]] on Strength (Athletics) checks to climb.

This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors.
