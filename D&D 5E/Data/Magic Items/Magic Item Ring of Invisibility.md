# Ring of Invisibility

_Ring, Legendary (Requires Attunement)_

While wearing this ring, you can take a [[Term Magic]] action to give yourself the [[Term Invisible]] condition. You remain Invisible until the ring is removed or until you take a [[Term Bonus Action]] to become visible again.
