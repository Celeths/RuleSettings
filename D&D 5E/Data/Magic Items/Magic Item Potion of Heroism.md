# Potion of Heroism

_Potion, Rare_

When you drink this potion, you gain 10 [[Term Temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 hour. For the same duration, you are under the effect of the [[Spell Bless]] spell (no [[Term Concentration]] required).

This potion's blue liquid bubbles and steams as if boiling.
