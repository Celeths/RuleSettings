# Scimitar of Speed

_Weapon (Scimitar), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)_

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, you can make one attack with it as a [[Term Bonus Action]] on each of your turns.
