# Arrow-Catching Shield

_Armor (Shield), Rare (Requires Attunement)_

You gain a +2 bonus to [[Term Armor Class]] against ranged attack rolls while you wield this Shield. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.

Whenever an attacker makes a ranged attack roll against a target within 5 feet of you, you can take a [[Term Reaction]] to become the target of the attack instead.
