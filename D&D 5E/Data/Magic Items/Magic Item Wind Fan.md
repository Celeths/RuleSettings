# Wind Fan

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon_

While holding this fan, you can cast [[Spell Gust of Wind]] (save DC 13) from it. Each subsequent time the fan is used before the next dawn, it has a cumulative 20 percent chance of not working; if the fan fails to work, it tears into useless, nonmagical tatters.
