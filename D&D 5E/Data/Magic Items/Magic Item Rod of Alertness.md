# Rod of Alertness

_Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)_

This rod has the following properties.

_**Alertness.**_ While holding the rod, you have [[Term Advantage]] on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on [[Term Initiative]] rolls.

_**Spells.**_ While holding the rod, you can cast the following spells from it:

- [[Spell Detect Evil and Good]]
- [[Spell Detect Magic]]
- [[Spell Detect Poison and Disease]]
- [[Spell See Invisibility]]

_**Protective Aura.**_ As a [[Term Magic]] action, you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod's head sheds [[Term Bright Light]] in a 60-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet. While in that Bright Light, you and your allies gain a +1 bonus to [[Term Armor Class]] and saving throws and can sense the location of any [[Term Invisible]] creature that is also in the Bright Light.

The rod's head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes or when a creature takes a [[Term Magic]] action to pull the rod from the ground. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
