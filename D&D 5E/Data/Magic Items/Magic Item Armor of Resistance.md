# Armor of Resistance

_Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (Requires Attunement)_

You have [[Term Resistance]] to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The GM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the table.

|      5 | Lightning     |

|      8 | Psychic       |
|      9 | Radiant       |
