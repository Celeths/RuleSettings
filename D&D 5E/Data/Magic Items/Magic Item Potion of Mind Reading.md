# Potion of Mind Reading

_Potion, Rare_

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Spell Detect Thoughts]] spell (save DC 13) for 10 minutes (no [[Term Concentration]] required).

This potion's dense, purple liquid has an ovoid cloud of pink floating in it.
