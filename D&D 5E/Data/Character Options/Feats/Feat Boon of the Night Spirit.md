# Boon of the Night Spirit

_Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Ability Score Increase.**_ Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

_**Merge with Shadows.**_ While within [[Term Dim Light]] or [[Term Darkness]], you can give yourself the [[Term Invisible]] condition as a [[Term Bonus Action]]. The condition ends on you immediately after you take an action, a Bonus Action, or a [[Term Reaction]].

_**Shadowy Form.**_ While within [[Term Dim Light]] or [[Term Darkness]], you have [[Term Resistance]] to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.
