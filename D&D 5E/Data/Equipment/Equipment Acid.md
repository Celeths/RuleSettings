# Acid

**Cost:** 25 GP

When you take the [[Term Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a vial of Acid. [[Term Target]] one creature or object you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d6 Acid damage.
