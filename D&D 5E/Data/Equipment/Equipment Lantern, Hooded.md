# Lantern, Hooded

**Cost:** 5 GP

A Hooded Lantern burns Oil as fuel to cast [[Term Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. As a [[Term Bonus Action]], you can lower the hood, reducing the light to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.
