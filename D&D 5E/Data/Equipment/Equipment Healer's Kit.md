# Healer's Kit

**Cost:** 5 GP

A Healer's Kit has ten uses. As a [[Term Utilize]] action, you can expend one of its uses to stabilize an [[Term Unconscious]] creature that has 0 [[Term Hit Points]] without needing to make a Wisdom (Medicine) check.
