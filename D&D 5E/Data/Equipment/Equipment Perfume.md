# Perfume

**Cost:** 5 GP

Perfume comes in a 4-ounce vial. For 1 hour after applying Perfume to yourself, you have [[Term Advantage]] on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to influence an [[Term Indifferent]] Humanoid within 5 feet of yourself.
