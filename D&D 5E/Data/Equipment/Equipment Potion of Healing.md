# Potion of Healing

**Cost:** 50 GP

This potion is a magic item. As a [[Term Bonus Action]], you can drink it or administer it to another creature within 5 feet of yourself. The creature that drinks the magical red fluid in this vial regains 2d4 + 2 [[Term Hit Points]].
