# Size

A creature or an object belongs to a size category: Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, or Gargantuan. A creature's size determines how much space the creature occupies in combat. An object's size affects its [[Term Hit Points]]. _See also_ "[[Term Breaking Objects]]" and "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
