# Advantage

If you have Advantage on a D20 Test, roll two d20s, and use the higher roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one Advantage, and Advantage and [[Term Disadvantage]] on the same roll cancel each other. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("[[Term D20 Test]]s").
