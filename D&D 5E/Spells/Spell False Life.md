# False Life

_Level 1 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of alcohol)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You gain 2d4 + 4 [[Term Temporary Hit Points]].

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You gain 5 additional [[Term Temporary Hit Points]] for each spell slot level above 1.
