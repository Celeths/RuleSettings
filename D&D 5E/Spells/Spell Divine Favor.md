# Divine Favor

_Level 1 Transmutation (Paladin)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

Until the spell ends, your attacks with weapons deal an extra 1d4 Radiant damage on a hit.
