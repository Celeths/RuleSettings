# Freedom of Movement

_Level 4 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[Term Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target's [[Term Speed]] nor cause the target to have the [[Term Paralyzed]] or [[Term Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a [[Term Swim Speed]] equal to its Speed.

In addition, the target can spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as manacles or a creature imposing the [[Term Grappled]] condition on it.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
