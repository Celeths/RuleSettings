# Beacon of Hope

_Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has [[Term Advantage]] on Wisdom saving throws and [[Term Death Saving Throw]]s and regains the maximum number of [[Term Hit Points]] possible from any healing.
