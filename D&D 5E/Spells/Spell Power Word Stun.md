# Power Word Stun

_Level 8 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range. If the target has 150 [[Term Hit Points]] or fewer, it has the [[Term Stunned]] condition. Otherwise, its [[Term Speed]] is 0 until the start of your next turn.

The [[Term Stunned]] target makes a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success.
