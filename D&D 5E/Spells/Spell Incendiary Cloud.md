# Incendiary Cloud

_Level 8 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

A swirling cloud of embers and smoke fills a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] centered on a point within range. The cloud's area is [[Term Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (like that created by [[Spell Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

When the cloud appears, each creature in it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d8 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the [[Term Sphere]] moves into its space and when it enters the Sphere or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The cloud moves 10 feet away from you in a direction you choose at the start of each of your turns.
