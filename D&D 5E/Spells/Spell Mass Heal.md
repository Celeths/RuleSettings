# Mass Heal

_Level 9 Abjuration (Cleric)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A flood of healing energy flows from you into creatures around you. You restore up to 700 [[Term Hit Points]], divided as you choose among any number of creatures that you can see within range. Creatures healed by this spell also have the [[Term Blinded]], [[Term Deafened]], and [[Term Poisoned]] conditions removed from them.
