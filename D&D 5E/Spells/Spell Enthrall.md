# Enthrall

_Level 2 Enchantment (Bard, Warlock)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a Wisdom saving throw. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a -10 penalty to Wisdom (Perception) checks and [[Term Passive Perception]] until the spell ends.
