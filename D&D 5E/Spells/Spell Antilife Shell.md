# Antilife Shell

_Level 5 Abjuration (Druid)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

An aura extends from you in a 10-foot [[Term Emanation]] for the duration. The aura prevents creatures other than Constructs and Undead from passing or reaching through it. An affected creature can cast spells or make attacks with Ranged or [[Term Reach]] weapons through the barrier.

If you move so that an affected creature is forced to pass through the barrier, the spell ends.
