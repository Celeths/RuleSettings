# Stoneskin

_Level 4 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has [[Term Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
