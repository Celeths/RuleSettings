# Alert

_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Initiative Proficiency.**_ When you roll [[Term Initiative]], you can add your Proficiency Bonus to the roll.

_**Initiative Swap.**_ Immediately after you roll [[Term Initiative]], you can swap your Initiative with the Initiative of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition.
