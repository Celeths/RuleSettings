# Orc

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 6-7 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet

As an Orc, you have these special traits.

_**Adrenaline Rush.**_ You can take the [[Term Dash]] action as a [[Term Bonus Action]]. When you do so, you gain a number of [[Term Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or [[Term Long Rest]].

_**Darkvision.**_ You have [[Term Darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet.

_**Relentless Endurance.**_ When you are reduced to 0 [[Term Hit Points]] but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Term Long Rest]].
