# Grappler

_General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Ability Score Increase.**_ Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

_**Punch and Grab.**_ When you hit a creature with an [[Term Unarmed Strike]] as part of the [[Term Attack]] action on your turn, you can use both the [[Term Damage]] and the Grapple option. You can use this benefit only once per turn.

_**Attack Advantage.**_ You have [[Term Advantage]] on attack rolls against a creature [[Term Grappled]] by you.

_**Fast Wrestler.**_ You don't have to spend extra movement to move a creature [[Term Grappled]] by you if the creature is your size or smaller.
