# Crystal Ball of Telepathy

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)_

While touching this crystal orb, you can cast [[Spell Scrying]] (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you can communicate telepathically with creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You can also cast [[Spell Suggestion]] (save DC 17) through the sensor on one of those creatures. You don't need to concentrate on this [[Spell Suggestion]] to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if [[Spell Scrying]] ends. You can't cast [[Spell Suggestion]] in this way again until the next dawn.
