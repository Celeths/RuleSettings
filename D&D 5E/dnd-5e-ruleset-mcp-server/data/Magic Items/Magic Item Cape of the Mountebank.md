# Cape of the Mountebank

_Wondrous Item, Rare_

This cape smells faintly of brimstone. While wearing it, you can use it to cast [[Spell Dimension Door]] as a [[Term Magic]] action. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

When you teleport with that spell, you leave behind a cloud of smoke. The space you left is [[Term Lightly Obscured]] by that smoke until the end of your next turn.
