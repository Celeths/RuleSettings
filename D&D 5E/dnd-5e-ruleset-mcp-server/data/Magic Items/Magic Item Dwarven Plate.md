# Dwarven Plate

_Armor (Half Plate Armor or Plate Armor), Very Rare_

While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to [[Term Armor Class]]. In addition, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can take a [[Term Reaction]] to reduce the distance you are moved by up to 10 feet.
