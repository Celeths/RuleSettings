# Holy Avenger

_Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Paladin)_

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with it, that creature takes an extra 2d10 Radiant damage.

While you hold the drawn weapon, it creates a 10-foot [[Term Emanation]] originating from you. You and all creatures [[Term Friendly]] to you in the Emanation have [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. If you have 17 or more levels in the Paladin class, the size of the Emanation increases to 30 feet.
