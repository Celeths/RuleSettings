# Gloves of Missile Snaring

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

If you're hit by an attack roll made with a Ranged or Thrown weapon while wearing these gloves, you can take a [[Term Reaction]] to reduce the damage by 1d10 plus your Dexterity modifier if you have a free hand. If you reduce the damage to 0, you can catch the ammunition or weapon if it is small enough for you to hold in that hand.
