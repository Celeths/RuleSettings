# Gem of Seeing

_Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)_

This gem has 3 charges. As a [[Term Magic]] action, you can expend 1 charge. For the next 10 minutes, you have [[Term Truesight]] out to 120 feet when you peer through the gem.

The gem regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
