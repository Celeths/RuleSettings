# Potion of Gaseous Form

_Potion, Rare_

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Spell Gaseous Form]] spell for 1 hour (no [[Term Concentration]] required) or until you end the effect as a [[Term Bonus Action]].

This potion's container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water.
