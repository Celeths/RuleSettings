# Periapt of Health

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

While wearing this pendant, you can take a [[Term Magic]] action to regain 2d4 + 2 [[Term Hit Points]]. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Term Poisoned]] condition while you wear this pendant.
