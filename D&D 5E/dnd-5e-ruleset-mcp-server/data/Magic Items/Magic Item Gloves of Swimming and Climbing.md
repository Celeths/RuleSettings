# Gloves of Swimming and Climbing

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

While wearing these gloves, you have a [[Term Climb Speed]] and a [[Term Swim Speed]] equal to your [[Term Speed]], and you gain a +5 bonus to Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb or swim.
