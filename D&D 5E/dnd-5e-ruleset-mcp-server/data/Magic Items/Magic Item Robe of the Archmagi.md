# Robe of the Archmagi

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard)_

This elegant garment is made from exquisite cloth and adorned with runes.

You gain these benefits while wearing the robe.

_**Armor.**_ If you aren't wearing armor, your base [[Term Armor Class]] is 15 plus your Dexterity modifier.

_**Magic Resistance.**_ You have [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

_**War Mage.**_ Your spell save DC and spell attack bonus each increase by 2.
