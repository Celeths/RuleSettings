# Cloak of the Bat

_Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)_

While wearing this cloak, you have [[Term Advantage]] on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. In an area of [[Term Dim Light]] or [[Term Darkness]], you can grip the edges of the cloak and use it to gain a [[Term Fly Speed]] of 40 feet. If you ever fail to grip the cloak's edges while flying in this way, or if you are no longer in Dim Light or Darkness, you lose this Fly [[Term Speed]].

While wearing the cloak in an area of [[Term Dim Light]] or [[Term Darkness]], you can cast [[Spell Polymorph]] on yourself, shape-shifting into a **[[Monster Bat]]**. While in that form, you retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores. The cloak can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
