# Mind Blank

_Level 8 Abjuration (Bard, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 24 hours

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has [[Term Immunity]] to Psychic damage and the [[Term Charmed]] condition. The target is also unaffected by anything that would sense its emotions or alignment, read its thoughts, or magically detect its location, and no spell — not even [[Spell Wish]] — can gather information about the target, observe it remotely, or control its mind.
