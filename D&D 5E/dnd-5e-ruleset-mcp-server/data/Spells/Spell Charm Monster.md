# Charm Monster

_Level 4 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

One creature you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. It does so with [[Term Advantage]] if you or your allies are fighting it. On a failed save, the target has the [[Term Charmed]] condition until the spell ends or until you or your allies damage it. The Charmed creature is [[Term Friendly]] to you. When the spell ends, the target knows it was Charmed by you.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
