# Barkskin

_Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of bark)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target's skin assumes a bark-like appearance, and the target has an [[Term Armor Class]] of 17 if its AC is lower than that.
