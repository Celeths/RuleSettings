# Heal

_Level 6 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. Positive energy washes through the target, restoring 70 [[Term Hit Points]]. This spell also ends the [[Term Blinded]], [[Term Deafened]], and [[Term Poisoned]] conditions on the target.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The healing increases by 10 for each spell slot level above 6.
