# Befuddlement

_Level 8 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a key ring with no keys)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You blast the mind of a creature that you can see within range. The target makes an Intelligence saving throw.

On a failed save, the target takes 10d12 Psychic damage and can't cast spells or take the [[Term Magic]] action. At the end of every 30 days, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on a success. The effect can also be ended by the [[Spell Greater Restoration]], [[Spell Heal]], or [[Spell Wish]] spell.

On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only.
