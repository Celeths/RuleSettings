# Healing Word

_Level 1 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Bonus Action]]
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature of your choice that you can see within range regains [[Term Hit Points]] equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The healing increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
