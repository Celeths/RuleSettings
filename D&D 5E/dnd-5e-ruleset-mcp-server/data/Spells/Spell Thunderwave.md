# Thunderwave

_Level 1 Evocation (Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a wave of thunderous energy. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Term Cube]] originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

In addition, unsecured objects that are entirely within the [[Term Cube]] are pushed 10 feet away from you, and a thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
