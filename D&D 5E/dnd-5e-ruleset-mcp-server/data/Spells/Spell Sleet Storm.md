# Sleet Storm

_Level 3 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature umbrella)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Until the spell ends, sleet falls in a 40-foot-tall, 20-foot-radius [[Term Cylinder]] centered on a point you choose within range. The area is [[Term Heavily Obscured]], and exposed flames in the area are doused.

Ground in the [[Term Cylinder]] is [[Term Difficult Terrain]]. When a creature enters the Cylinder for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a

Dexterity saving throw or have the [[Term Prone]] condition and lose [[Term Concentration]].
