# Protection from Poison

_Level 2 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[Term Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition, and it has [[Term Resistance]] to Poison damage.
