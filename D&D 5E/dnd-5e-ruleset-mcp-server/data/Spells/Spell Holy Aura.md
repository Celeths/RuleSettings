# Holy Aura

_Level 8 Abjuration (Cleric)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a reliquary worth 1,000+ GP)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

For the duration, you emit an aura in a 30-foot [[Term Emanation]]. While in the aura, creatures of your choice have [[Term Advantage]] on all saving throws, and other creatures have [[Term Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against them. In addition, when a Fiend or an Undead hits an affected creature with a melee attack roll, the attacker must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[Term Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
