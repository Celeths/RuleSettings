# Protection from Energy

_Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [[Term Resistance]] to one damage type of your choice: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder.
