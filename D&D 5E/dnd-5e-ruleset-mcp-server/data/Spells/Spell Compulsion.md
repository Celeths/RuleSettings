# Compulsion

_Level 4 Enchantment (Bard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Each creature of your choice that you can see within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Term Charmed]] condition until the spell ends.

For the duration, you can take a [[Term Bonus Action]] to designate a direction that is horizontal to you. Each [[Term Charmed]] target must use as much of its movement as possible to move in that direction on its next turn, taking the safest route. After moving in this way, a target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
