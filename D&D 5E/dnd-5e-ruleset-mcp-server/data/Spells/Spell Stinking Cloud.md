# Stinking Cloud

_Level 3 Conjuration (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a rotten egg)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Term Sphere]] of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is [[Term Heavily Obscured]]. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by [[Spell Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the [[Term Sphere]] must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[Term Poisoned]] condition until the end of the current turn. While Poisoned in this way, the creature can't take an action or a [[Term Bonus Action]].
