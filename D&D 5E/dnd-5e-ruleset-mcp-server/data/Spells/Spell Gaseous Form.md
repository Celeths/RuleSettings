# Gaseous Form

_Level 3 Transmutation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

- **Casting Time:** [[Term Action]]
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of gauze)
- **Duration:** [[Term Concentration]], up to 1 hour

A willing creature you touch shape-shifts, along with everything it's wearing and carrying, into a misty cloud for the duration. The spell ends on the target if it drops to 0 [[Term Hit Points]] or if it takes a [[Term Magic]] action to end the spell on itself.

While in this form, the target's only method of movement is a [[Term Fly Speed]] of 10 feet, and it can hover. The target can enter and occupy the space of another creature. The target has [[Term Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage; it has [[Term Immunity]] to the [[Term Prone]] condition; and it has [[Term Advantage]] on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. The target can pass through narrow openings, but it treats liquids as though they were solid surfaces.

The target can't talk or manipulate objects, and any objects it was carrying or holding can't be dropped, used, or otherwise interacted with. Finally, the target can't attack or cast spells.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3.
