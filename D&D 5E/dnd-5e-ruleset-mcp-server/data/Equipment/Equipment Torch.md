# Torch

**Cost:** 1 CP

A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting [[Term Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Term Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. When you take the [[Term Attack]] action, you can attack with the Torch, using it as a Simple Melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.
