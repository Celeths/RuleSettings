# Ice Devil

_Large Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil_

- **AC** 18
- **Initiative** +7 (17)
- **HP** 228 (24d10 + 96)
- **Speed** 40 ft.

|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|

| **Saving Throw** |     +5 |     +7 |     +9 |     +4 |     +7 |     +9 |

- **Skills** Insight +7, Perception +7, Persuasion +9
- **Immunities** Cold, Fire, Poison; [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Blindsight]] 120 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 17
- **Languages** Infernal; telepathy 120 ft.
- **CR** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)

## Traits

_**Diabolical Restoration.**_ If the devil dies outside the Nine Hells, its body disappears in sulfurous smoke, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its [[Term Hit Points]] somewhere in the Nine Hells.

_**Magic Resistance.**_ The devil has [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The devil makes three Ice Spear attacks. It can replace one attack with a Tail attack.

_**Ice Spear.**_ _Melee or Ranged [[Term Attack Roll]]:_ +10, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Cold damage. Until the end of its next turn, the target can't take a [[Term Bonus Action]] or [[Term Reaction]], its [[Term Speed]] decreases by 10 feet, and it can move or take one action on its turn, not both. _Hit or Miss:_ The spear magically returns to the devil's hand immediately after a ranged attack.

_**Tail.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +10, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 15 (3d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) Cold damage.

_**Ice Wall (Recharge 6).**_ The devil casts [[Spell Wall of Ice]] (level 8 version), requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17).
