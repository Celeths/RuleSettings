# Chuul

_Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil_

- **AC** 16
- **Initiative** +0 (10)
- **HP** 76 (9d10 + 27)
- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     19 |     10 |     16 |      5 |     11 |      5 |
| **Modifier**     |     +4 |     +0 |     +3 |     -3 |     +0 |     -3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +4 |     +0 |     +3 |     -3 |     +0 |     -3 |

- **Skills** Perception +4
- **Immunities** Poison; [[Term Poisoned]]
- **Senses** [[Term Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 14
- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
- **CR** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)

## Traits

_**Amphibious.**_ The chuul can breathe air and water.

_**Sense Magic.**_ The chuul senses magic within 120 feet of itself. This trait otherwise works like the [[Spell Detect Magic]] spell but isn't itself magical.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The chuul makes two Pincer attacks and uses Paralyzing Tentacles.

_**Pincer.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Term Grappled]] condition (escape DC 14) from one of two pincers.

_**Paralyzing Tentacles.**_ _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one creature [[Term Grappled]] by the chuul. _Failure:_ The target has the [[Term Poisoned]] condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. While Poisoned, the target has the [[Term Paralyzed]] condition.
