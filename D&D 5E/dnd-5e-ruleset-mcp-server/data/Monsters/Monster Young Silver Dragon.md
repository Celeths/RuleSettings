# Young Silver Dragon

_Large Dragon (Metallic), Lawful Good_

- **AC** 18
- **Initiative** +4 (14)
- **HP** 168 (16d10 + 80)
- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     23 |     10 |     21 |     14 |     11 |     19 |
| **Modifier**     |     +6 |     +0 |     +5 |     +2 |     +0 |     +4 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +6 |     +4 |     +5 |     +2 |     +4 |     +4 |

- **Skills** History +6, Perception +8, Stealth +4
- **Immunities** Cold
- **Senses** [[Term Blindsight]] 30 ft., [[Term Darkvision]] 120 ft.; [[Term Passive Perception]] 18
- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **CR** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The dragon makes three Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Paralyzing Breath.

_**Rend.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +10, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 15 (2d8 + 6) Slashing damage.

_**Cold Breath (Recharge 5-6).**_ _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot [[Term Cone]]. _Failure:_ 49 (11d8) Cold damage. _Success:_ Half damage.

_**Paralyzing Breath.**_ _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot [[Term Cone]]. _First Failure:_ The target has the [[Term Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn, when it repeats the save. _Second Failure:_ The target has the [[Term Paralyzed]] condition, and it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.
