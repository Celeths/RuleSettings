# Archmage

_Medium or Small Humanoid (Wizard), Neutral_

- **AC** 17
- **Initiative** +7 (17)
- **HP** 170 (31d8 + 31)
- **Speed** 30 ft.

|                  |   Str. |   Dex. |   Con. |   Int. |   Wis. |   Cha. |
|------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Score**        |     10 |     14 |     12 |     20 |     15 |     16 |
| **Modifier**     |     +0 |     +2 |     +1 |     +5 |     +2 |     +3 |
| **Saving Throw** |     +0 |     +2 |     +1 |     +9 |     +6 |     +3 |

- **Skills** Arcana +13, History +9, Perception +6
- **Immunities** Psychic; [[Term Charmed]] (with [[Spell Mind Blank]])
- **Gear** Wand
- **Senses** [[Term Passive Perception]] 16
- **Languages** Common plus five other languages
- **CR** 12 (XP 8,000; PB +4)

## Traits

_**Magic Resistance.**_ The archmage has [[Term Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The archmage makes four Arcane Burst attacks.

_**Arcane Burst.**_ _Melee or Ranged [[Term Attack Roll]]:_ +9, reach 5 ft. or range 150 ft. _Hit:_ 27 (4d10 + 5) Force damage.

_**Spellcasting.**_ The archmage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):

- **At Will:** [[Spell Detect Magic]], [[Spell Detect Thoughts]], [[Spell Disguise Self]], [[Spell Invisibility]], [[Spell Light]], [[Spell Mage Armor]] (included in AC), [[Spell Mage Hand]], [[Spell Prestidigitation]]
- **2/Day Each:** [[Spell Fly]], [[Spell Lightning Bolt]] (level 7 version)
- **1/Day Each:** [[Spell Cone of Cold]] (level 9 version), [[Spell Mind Blank]]  (cast before combat), [[Spell Scrying]], [[Spell Teleport]]

## Bonus Actions

_**Misty Step (3/Day).**_ The mage casts [[Spell Misty Step]], using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

## Reactions

_**Protective Magic (3/Day).**_ The archmage casts [[Spell Counterspell]] or [[Spell Shield]] in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
