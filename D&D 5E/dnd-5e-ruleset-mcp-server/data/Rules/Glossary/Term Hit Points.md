# Hit Points

Hit Points (HP) are a measure of how difficult it is to kill or destroy a creature or an object. [[Term Damage]] reduces Hit Points, and healing restores them. You can't have more Hit Points than your Hit Point maximum, and you can't have less than 0. _See also_ "[[Term Breaking Objects]]" and "Playing the Game" ("Damage and [[Term Healing]]").
