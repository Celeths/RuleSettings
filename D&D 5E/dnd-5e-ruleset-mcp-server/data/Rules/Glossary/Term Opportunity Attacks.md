# Opportunity Attacks

You can make an Opportunity [[Term Attack]] when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its action, its [[Term Bonus Action]], its [[Term Reaction]], or one of its speeds. To make the Opportunity Attack, take a Reaction to make one melee attack with a weapon or an [[Term Unarmed Strike]] against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach. _See also_ "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
