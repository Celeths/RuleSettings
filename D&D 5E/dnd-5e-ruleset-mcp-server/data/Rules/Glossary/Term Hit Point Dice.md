# Hit Point Dice

Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a player character's Hit Point maximum, as explained in "Character Creation." Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Term Short Rest]] to regain [[Term Hit Points]]. _See also_ "Short Rest."
